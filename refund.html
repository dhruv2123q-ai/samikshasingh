Returns & Refunds

Our policies for returns, exchanges, refunds and cancellations.

Last Updated: November 8, 2026

1. Our Policy

At Samiksha Store, customer satisfaction matters to us. If you receive a damaged, defective or incorrect product, please contact our support team so we can review your request.

2. Return Eligibility

Our proposed return window is 7 days from the date of delivery. To qualify for a return, the following conditions should generally be met:

  • The product must be unused and unworn.
  • Original tags, labels and packaging should be intact.
  • The product should be returned in its original condition.
  • Proof of purchase or order details must be provided.
  • The return request must be raised within the stated return window.

Some products may be excluded from returns for hygiene or other legitimate reasons. Any exclusions will be disclosed before purchase.

3. Damaged or Incorrect Products

If you receive a damaged, defective or incorrect item, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order details and clear photographs or video of the issue.

After reviewing the request, we may arrange a replacement, exchange or eligible refund, depending on the circumstances and product availability.

4. Exchange Policy

Size or product exchanges may be requested within 7 days of delivery, subject to eligibility and stock availability. The item must be unused, unworn and have its original tags and packaging.

Contact our support team before sending any product back. Exchanges are not confirmed until approved by our team.

5. Refund Process

Once an eligible returned product is received and inspected, we will notify you about the approval or rejection of your refund request.

  • Approved refunds will be processed using an appropriate refund method.
  • Refund processing time may depend on the payment provider or bank.
  • Shipping charges, if any, may not be refundable unless required by applicable law or the issue is attributable to us.
  • We will communicate the refund amount and method before processing.

6. Cash on Delivery (COD) – ₹99 Advance

Important: For COD orders, a ₹99 upfront payment is required at checkout as a delivery/confirmation charge. The remaining order balance is payable to the delivery partner at the time of delivery.

  • The ₹99 payment must be completed before the COD order is confirmed.
  • The remaining payable amount will be shown during checkout or in the order confirmation.
  • The ₹99 charge is separate from the remaining COD balance and will be displayed clearly before placing the order.
  • If an order is cancelled before dispatch, the customer may request a review of the ₹99 payment.
  • If an order has already been dispatched, cancellation and refund of the advance charge will be reviewed based on the actual shipping costs and applicable law.

7. Order Cancellation

You may request cancellation before your order is dispatched by contacting our support team as soon as possible.

Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation may no longer be possible. Please contact us for assistance with any eligible return request.

8. How to Request a Return or Refund

To raise a request, contact our support team with:

  • Your full name and order number.
  • Registered mobile number or email address.
  • Reason for the return, exchange or refund.
  • Photographs or video evidence, where relevant.
